Who is responsible

Yulovo is published by Timur Egamov, an individual developer (“we”, “us”). We decide how your personal data is processed and are responsible for it under Vietnam’s personal data protection law. Contact: timuregamov2000@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your memories (photos, videos, title, story, dates, place name, address and coordinates, the people and tags you add), your trail and your settings are stored on your phone. If you do not sign in, they stay there and we receive none of them.

You can sign in with Apple or Google. We then receive an account identifier and your email address (or a private relay address, if you hide your email with Apple), and the name and profile picture Apple or Google pass on. They become your display name and picture, which friends see; you can change both at any time.

When you are signed in, your memories, photos, videos and trail are synced to our servers, so they are safe when you change phones and you can share them with friends. Memories you made before signing in move into your account. Connections are encrypted, and access rules let only you read your own data, apart from what friends see as described below. We never see or store a password.

What friends see

You add friends with an invite code or link. Anyone who has your code or link can see your display name before adding you. You can make a new code at any time; the old one then stops working.

Your friends see your display name and profile picture, and every memory of yours that is not set to “Only me”: its title, photos, place name and exact pin, dates, the friends you tagged, and the hearts and comments on it with the names and pictures of the people who left them.

Friends never see your stories, the people and tags you type into a memory, or memories set to “Only me”.

Your trail (where you have been, the uncovered fog) is not shared unless you turn on “Share where I’ve been” in Settings → Privacy. Friends then see the uncovered areas, rounded to about 100 m. This is off until you turn it on.

Friends can heart and comment on your memories, tag you in theirs and add photos to memories they tagged you in. A memory you are tagged in appears on your map. Your hearts, comments and the photos you add can be seen, with your name and picture, by everyone who can see that memory, including the owner’s other friends.

Blocking and reports

When you block someone, the block is stored on our server so it can be enforced.

When you report a memory, comment, photo or person, we receive who sent the report, what it is about, the reason, any note you add and a copy of the reported content, so we can review it even if the author deletes it. We use reports only to deal with abuse.

Notifications

Push notifications are sent only if you allow them. We then store your device’s push token, the app’s language and the platform, linked to your account.

Notifications about friends’ activity pass through our push notification provider and Apple. They contain the friend’s name, the memory’s title and, for a comment, up to its first 80 characters.

“On this day” reminders are scheduled on your phone; their content never leaves it.

Camera, microphone and photos

Photos for memories are taken with the camera inside the app. With Yulovo Plus you can also film short videos with sound; the microphone is used only while you film.

Your photo library opens only when you choose a profile picture or add photos to a friend’s memory, and the app reads only the photos you pick.

Location

When you take a photo, the app reads your location to pin the memory where you are. This needs location access while you use the app.

With Explore mode on, the app also records your location in the background to uncover the fog on your map. You can turn Explore mode off in Settings, or change location access in iOS Settings, at any time.

Under Vietnamese law, location data is sensitive personal data. We process it only with your consent, which you give by allowing location access and turning on Explore mode, and only for these features.

Service providers

To run the app we use service providers for: storing accounts and synced data (Singapore); push notifications and app updates (USA); finding the place name for a memory’s coordinates (Germany); map tiles (built from OpenStreetMap data, servers in several countries); and managing subscriptions (USA). Each receives only what its task needs, such as your IP address, the coordinates being looked up or the map area you load, and is bound to protect it at least as well as this policy does. We use them only for the purposes above; by using these features you agree to this transfer of data abroad.

Yulovo Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card. We keep only whether you have Plus, which plan and until when.

Signing in with Apple or Google, and buying through the App Store, are also covered by Apple’s and Google’s own privacy policies.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling or renting of data. This version of the app contains no analytics. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first.

We do not look at your memories in the normal running of the service. We look at content only when it is reported to us, when you ask us for help with it, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep data

Your account data (memories, photos, videos, trail, profile, comments, Plus status): for as long as your account exists, or until you delete them. A memory you delete is erased from the server on the next sync.

Push tokens: until you sign out, the token stops working, someone else signs in on that phone, or you delete your account.

Reports: as long as needed to handle them and to deal with repeated abuse. After an account is deleted, its reports are no longer linked to it.

Our providers keep technical logs and backups for a short period, after which they expire.

Deleting your data

A memory you delete is deleted for good. There is no trash.

Settings → Account → Delete account and data deletes, straight away, everything linked to your account from our servers and from this phone: your account, memories, photos, videos, trail, profile, friendships, comments, hearts, the photos you added to friends’ memories, your tags in them, your activity and your push tokens. Backups may keep a copy for a short period until they are replaced.

Your rights

Under Vietnamese law you have the right to be informed, to give or refuse consent, to access your data and get a copy, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict its processing, to object, to withdraw consent, to complain to the authorities and to claim compensation. If you live elsewhere, for example in the EU or the UK, you have similar rights under your local law, including data portability.

In the app: edit a memory to correct it. Write to us (below) for a copy of all your data and photos. Delete as described above. Withdraw consent by turning off Explore mode or notifications, changing permissions in iOS Settings, signing out or deleting your account. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was processed before.

For anything else, write to timuregamov2000@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm the request comes from your account. We reply within 30 days at most, and sooner where the law requires a shorter time. You can also complain to the data protection authority.

Children

Yulovo is not intended for children under 13. If you are under 16, you need the consent of a parent or guardian to use the app and to create an account. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, or a child under 16 without that consent, we will delete it. Parents can contact us at any time.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the app changes how it handles data. The effective date is at the top. For important changes we will also tell you in the app before they apply.
